A logic-based randomizer for Bionic Commando (NES, USA). Bring your own ROM and get back a randomized ROM to play in your emulator of choice. Your ROM never leaves your computer.

Found a bug or a misleading hint, got stuck, or have an idea? Please report it on GitHub, with your seed link (Copy Link, after you generate) so it can be reproduced. Screenshots help too, but please don't upload your ROM.
